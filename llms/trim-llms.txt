# Trim

> Trim is a consumer fintech company (founded 2015, backed by Version One Ventures) whose automated personal finance assistant finds and cancels unwanted subscriptions, negotiates bills, and automates savings. Trim was acquired by OneMain Financial and the product now lives on as OneMain MyMoney (asktrim.com redirects to onemainmymoney.com). Trim/MyMoney is a consumer application with no public developer API.
